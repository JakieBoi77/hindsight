The sound effects are copied unchanged from the 2D cosmetics PoC (`2d-poc-with-cosmetics-and-minigames`), which trimmed and levelled them with its `Tools/SoundFetch/fetch_sounds.py`.

Original art reused from the 2D prototype (`feat/poc-eye-drops`, generated by its `Tools/ArtGen` script, project licence): the SVG icons in `Assets/_Project/Art/Icons/`. Made for this project in the same style: `replay`, `gear`, `coin`, `close`, `shirt` and `spin`.

Generated in code (no external art): the room shell, eye chart, eye-drop bottle, ophthalmoscope, close-up eye model, the ceiling picture (smiley sun, rainbow and clouds, painted by `Editor/CeilingArt.cs`), the drops chart on the wall (painted by `Editor/ChartArt.cs`), all the characters (the scouts, original designs in the same genre as PEAK: bodies, faces painted by `Editor/FaceArtPainter.cs`, fits and hats, built by `Editor/BeanBuilder.cs`), and the UI shape sprites in `Assets/_Project/Art/UI/`.
